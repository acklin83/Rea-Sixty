What ORC does

ORC turns an SSL UF1 into a remote control for RME TotalMix FX. The fader, the pots and the keys move TotalMix directly, and the two screens show what TotalMix is doing: channel names and colours, levels, the EQ, reverb and echo. ORC runs in the menu bar and needs no DAW.

With an SSL UF8 next to it, the UF8 shows eight TotalMix channels on its faders, or drives Philips Hue lamps and DynaMount mic stands, and its soft keys switch OBS scenes (the UF8). The UF8 works on its own too.

ORC is free.

What you need

  • A Mac with macOS {{minMacos}} or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
  • An SSL UF1, an SSL UF8, or both, connected by USB.
  • An RME interface with TotalMix FX 2.1 or later. ORC talks Global OSC, which arrived with 2.1.

SSL 360° has to be closed while ORC uses the UF1. Only one program can hold the surface at a time.

Setting up

In TotalMix

  1. Under Settings, OSC, set Compatibility Mode to Global OSC, and switch Enable OSC Control on under Options.
  2. Pick a free remote in the OSC settings and tick In Use.
  3. Set its IP to {{host}}, Port incoming to {{sendPort}} and Port outgoing to {{recvPort}}. These are ORC's numbers out of the box.
  4. For the level meters on the UF1, switch Send Peak Level on for that remote. Without it everything else works and the meters stay empty.

ORC

  1. Open the disk image and drag ORC to Applications.
  2. Quit SSL 360°, connect the UF1, start ORC. Its menu bar item says who holds the UF1 and whether TotalMix answers (the menu bar).
  3. If you want ORC there whenever the Mac is on, tick Open at login in the settings window, page General.

A different port or a TotalMix on another machine goes in the settings window, page General.

ORC and Rea-Sixty

Rea-Sixty, my REAPER extension for SSL surfaces, shows the same TotalMix view on the UF1 inside REAPER: hold SHIFT, press MODE, and pick RME. It runs the code ORC runs, so the surface works as this page describes, with two differences: MODE opens REAPER's mode menu, and a transport key runs the action you gave it in ORC, its lamp in ORC's colours, or else stays REAPER's transport, lamp included.

Two Rea-Sixty built-ins put this on any key or a Stream Deck button: UF1 Mode: RME switches the UF1 to this view and back, RME: Ask TotalMix again does what the button of the same name on the General page does.

Who holds the UF1

When REAPER starts, Rea-Sixty asks ORC for the UF1 and the UF8, and ORC lets go of both surfaces, the TotalMix port, and its connections to Hue, OBS and the DynaMount stands. REAPER opens the surfaces once ORC has confirmed. When REAPER quits, ORC takes everything back. The stands report no position of their own, so their positions go across with the surfaces, for every stand set up with the same IP address in both programs. The menu bar then reads handed over to REAPER for as long as REAPER has it. Rea-Sixty's setting Take the UF1 over from ORC (Settings, Connected devices) switches this off, and then REAPER leaves the UF1 with ORC.

ORC can only take the UF1 back after REAPER if it is running, so with both on one Mac, Open at login is worth ticking.

In REAPER, Shift and LAYER 1, 2 or 3 on the UF8 open the same three modes as in ORC (the UF8), and the same key again goes back to REAPER. The TotalMix mode needs ORC installed.

One set of settings

ORC keeps the settings and Rea-Sixty reads them: pot targets, soft-key banks, colours, STRIP pages. Change them in ORC and REAPER picks them up. Without ORC installed, Rea-Sixty offers no TotalMix view at all.

Hue, DynaMount and OBS are kept by each program on its own, so each works without the other, and they are brought in step at every handover: when REAPER takes the surfaces, Rea-Sixty takes every setting that was changed in ORC since; when REAPER quits, ORC takes every one changed in REAPER. The newer change wins. A setting nobody has changed since this came in is ORC's on the first handover. Import from REAPER on the UF8 Modes page and Import from ORC on Rea-Sixty's Hue, OBS and DynaMount pages copy everything at once.

Rows, channels and the submix

TotalMix has three rows of channels: inputs, playbacks and outputs. ORC adds a fourth, FX, below the outputs, with two channels: Reverb and Echo. The nav cross steps from row to row, top to bottom and round again.

A row on the UF1 shows the channels TotalMix shows, in TotalMix' order. Hide a channel in TotalMix and ORC skips it. On the output row the control room comes first: Phones 1 to 4, Main B, Main, then the other outputs. A channel that carries a control-room role is named by the role, so the output you use as Phones 1 reads Phones 1.

The submix

An input or a playback has no single level in TotalMix. It has one level in every output's mix. ORC works the way TotalMix does when you click an output: the output selected last is the submix, and the fader, the pan pot and SOLO of an input or a playback act on that output's mix. The small screen shows which one, as > Main for example. Nav left and right step the submix through the visible outputs.

STRIP: one channel in detail

Push the CHANNEL encoder and the UF1 shows the channel on the fader in detail: its settings in TotalMix, four pots and four keys to a page. Push again to go back to the overview.

  • < and > step through the pages; the header shows the page and, for example, 3/9.
  • A page appears only when the channel has what is on it, as TotalMix reports it. A line input without a preamp has no gain, a playback has no EQ.
  • Pages next to each other for the same rows are packed: the parameters a channel has move up to fill four pots, so no page is half empty. The screen names every pot, so nothing is hidden by it moving. Stereo is the exception: it is always the fourth key of the first page.
  • On the FX row, Reverb and Echo have their own pages. Which reverb controls show depends on the reverb type, as in TotalMix' FX window (reverb types).
  • A push on a pot sets it back to the value in the last column. Where the column is empty, the push leaves the value alone.

The page list below is ORC's out of the box, read from the code this version runs.

Reverb types

Every type has PreDelay, Low Cut, Smooth and Width. The rest depends on the type, as TotalMix' own FX window shows it. Turning the type pot loads the type's own values in TotalMix, and ORC reads them back at once.

The UF8's channel view

PLUG-IN in TotalMix, or a push on the CHANNEL encoder, puts the selected channel's STRIP pages on the UF8. Press it again to go back to the overview.

  • The soft keys pick the page: each key is one page, and the page showing is lit. PAGE ◄ ► step to the previous and next page, and are lit where there is one.
  • V-Pots 1-4 are the page's controls, V-Pots 5-8 its switches: a push switches, a turn does nothing. The bottom line names each one and shows its value.
  • On the FX row, Reverb and Echo spread their controls over all eight V-Pots, eight to a page, so every reverb type but Envelope fits on one page. Soft key 8 switches the effect on and off: bright while on, dim while off. CUT on its strip does the same.
  • A push on a control sets it back to its default, the last column of the page list under STRIP.
  • The faders stay the eight channels' levels. The UF8 shows values as numbers; the EQ curve is the UF1's.

Hue, DynaMount and OBS on the UF8

Hue

Each lamp or zone set up on the page UF8 Modes sits on one strip, from the left or from the right. Strips without a lamp are dark. What each control does is under its own heading above.

DynaMount

Each stand set up on the page UF8 Modes sits on one strip. A preset is where every stand is at one moment; the soft keys are the eight presets. A preset finds each stand by its IP address, and a stand the preset does not know stays where it is.

OBS

OBS's scenes are a kind of soft-key bank: put OBS scenes on a bank on the page Soft Keys, and in TotalMix its key on V-POT to 5 brings them up, on the UF8 and on the UF1.

MoreMe: headphone mixes on phones

With MoreMe on, every musician opens a web page on a phone or tablet, picks a headphone mix (Phones 1 to 4 in TotalMix) and sets the level, pan, mute and solo of each source in it, plus the mix's master level and mute. The page shows the channel names and colours from TotalMix and follows the phone's language: German, English, French, Italian or Rumantsch, otherwise English. Each phone can pick another language in the page's settings (⚙), Klingon too. Nothing is installed on the phones.

MoreMe uses its own TotalMix remote, separate from the one the surfaces use. It keeps running when REAPER takes the surfaces over (ORC and Rea-Sixty), so the headphone mixes stay on the phones during a recording.

Setting it up

  1. In TotalMix, pick a second free remote in the OSC settings, tick In Use, IP {{host}}, Port incoming 7003, Port outgoing 7004. These are MoreMe's numbers out of the box. TotalMix opens a new remote's port when it starts, so restart TotalMix after setting it up.
  2. In ORC, settings window, page MoreMe, tick Headphone mixes in the browser. ORC makes a four-digit PIN.
  3. Give the musicians the address and the PIN from the menu bar, or let them scan the QR code. Phone and Mac have to be on the same network. A phone remembers the PIN and its mix.

Only one program can use a remote at a time. If another program already answers on 7003 and 7004, give MoreMe other ports, in TotalMix and on the MoreMe page alike.

Which channels the musicians see

The phones show the channels TotalMix sends: a channel you hide in TotalMix' layout does not show on them. ORC asks TotalMix again every three seconds, so a channel you hide or show appears or goes away on the phones a few seconds later. All headphone mixes show the same channels.

Taken headphones

A headphone mix in use on one phone is marked taken on the others. Tapping it asks whether to take it over; the phone that had it goes back to the list. A phone that drops off the network frees its mix within a minute.

Mute and solo

TotalMix has no mute for one source in one mix, so MoreMe's mute turns that source down to off and brings it back to the level it had. Moving the same fader in TotalMix ends the mute. Solo is TotalMix' own solo in that mix.

The PIN, and access from outside

Every phone has to enter the PIN before it sees anything. Five wrong PINs within ten minutes lock that address out for ten minutes. A new PIN sends every phone back to the PIN entry. The QR code has the address only, never the PIN.

Inside the studio network nothing needs setting up: the musicians scan the QR code in the menu, or type the address next to it.

Your own address from outside

For musicians who join from elsewhere, or a phone on mobile data, MoreMe can sit behind an address of your own, such as phones.example.com:

  1. Give the Mac a fixed address in your router (a DHCP reservation), so the forwarding below always finds it. The Mac must stay awake while the musicians mix.
  2. Point the name at your studio's public address in your domain's DNS.
  3. Run a reverse proxy with HTTPS on a device the internet can reach: a NAS, a router with a proxy, or a server. Forward the name to the Mac on MoreMe's port (8088 out of the box), with WebSocket support switched on for /ws. HTTPS is not optional here: without it the PIN crosses the internet as plain text.
  4. Without opening a port in the router, a tunnel service does the same: a program on the Mac keeps an encrypted connection out to the service, which serves your name.

The lock-out after five wrong PINs counts per address. Behind a proxy every phone arrives from the proxy, so MoreMe reads the phone's own address from X-Real-IP, or from the last entry of X-Forwarded-For when that is all the proxy sends. It believes these headers only from a proxy inside a private network; from anywhere else they could be forged.

Soft-key banks

In the overview the four keys above the large screen play a bank of eight keys, four at a time. < and > step through the banks, BANK ◄ ► pick the half. A bank holds either keys you set up yourself or one of three lists:

  • TotalMix snapshots: the eight snapshots by the names TotalMix gives them. A press loads one. The key of the active snapshot is lit.
  • TotalMix layouts: the eight layouts. A press loads one; the one loaded last is lit.
  • OBS scenes: the first eight scenes OBS reports, in OBS's order. A press switches to one; the scene on air is lit. Needs OBS set up on the page UF8 Modes.

On the UF8, V-POT to 5 pick banks 1 to 6, and its eight soft keys show a bank's two halves at once (soft keys).

Out of the box:

Every bank, key, label and action is set on the settings page Soft Keys. The transport keys are on the page UF1, the UF8's own keys on the page UF8.

HueFind bridge, then Pair and the bridge's link button within 30 seconds. Then up to eight lamps, rooms or zones, and eight scenes, each scene listed with its room and with the label its key shows; the label follows the scene you pick until you type one. Last, the V-Pot's two roles.

When something is off

The TotalMix line

It saysWhat it means, what to do
onlineAll good.
offTalk to TotalMix is off (page General).
waiting for TotalMixORC asks and nobody answers. TotalMix is not running, OSC is off, the remote is not In Use, or the ports do not match (setting up).
port … is in use by another programAnother program listens on ORC's port. Pick a different Listen on port and set the same number as the remote's Port outgoing in TotalMix.
could not open a socketmacOS refused ORC a network connection. Quit ORC and start it again.
host is not an IPv4 addressThe Host field holds something other than an address like 192.168.1.20.
TotalMix stopped answering (quit, or the remote is off)It was online and went quiet. Check that TotalMix is running and the remote is still In Use.

The UF1 line

It saysWhat it means, what to do
held by ORCAll good.
not open: handed over to REAPERRea-Sixty has the UF1 while REAPER runs (ORC and Rea-Sixty).
not open, with another reasonORC could not open the UF1. Is it connected and switched on? Is SSL 360° still running? ORC tries again every two seconds.

The UF8 line

It saysWhat it means, what to do
held by ORCAll good.
not open: handed over to REAPERRea-Sixty has the UF8 while REAPER runs.
REAPER cannot take it yet: update the REAPER extensionThe Rea-Sixty in REAPER is older than this ORC and may not pick the UF8 up. Update Rea-Sixty.
not open, with another reasonIs the UF8 connected and switched on? Is SSL 360° still running? ORC tries again every two seconds.

The MoreMe line

It saysWhat it means, what to do
offMoreMe is off (page MoreMe).
no phones connected, 1 phone connected, … phones connectedAll good: the page is served, TotalMix answers, and this many phones are past the PIN.
TotalMix does not answerMoreMe's remote is not set up in TotalMix, the ports do not match, or TotalMix was not restarted after setting it up (MoreMe). The phones say the same.
TotalMix port in use by another programAnother program listens on MoreMe's TotalMix listen on port. Pick another, in TotalMix and on the MoreMe page.
port … is in use by another programAnother program uses the page port. Pick another Page port.
no PIN setThe PIN file was removed. Untick and tick MoreMe again for a new one.
could not open a socketmacOS refused ORC a network connection. Quit ORC and start it again.

The phone shows Connecting to ORC when it cannot reach the Mac: wrong network, wrong address, or MoreMe is off. The first time a phone connects, macOS may ask whether ORC may accept incoming connections; allow it.
